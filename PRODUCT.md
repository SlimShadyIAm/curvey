# Curvey

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Desktop players joining friends through invite links for competitive curve-survival matches. Players steer continuously moving curves, avoid persistent trails, and use pickups to outlast opponents. The experience should feel faithful to Curve Crash while using original branding and UI assets.

## Confirmed release scope

- Invite-only online FFA rooms, 2–24 participants, one human player per browser. Hosts may add/remove automatically ready bots before matches and rematches; solo play supports one human plus up to 23 bots.
- Five fixed presets: Basic, None, Thin, Corner, Thorner.
- Core 12 powerups. Hosts choose preset, player capacity, and target score only.
- Guest names and free colors. No account required.
- Ready checks, round and match results, rematches, host transfer and kick.
- Lobby and between-round text chat, local mute. Eliminated players watch their existing match.
- A detected disconnect immediately eliminates the player; reconnection never resurrects that round.
- Later target: 32-player FFA, gated on load testing and arena/standings usability.

## Deferred

Public discovery, dedicated spectators, teams, shared keyboard, mobile gameplay, accounts, rankings, paid cosmetics, remaining powerups, advanced room settings, production input recording and replay viewers.

## Brand personality

Competitive, precise, restrained. Gameplay and readable state take priority over decoration. Original name: Curvey.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing landing-page funnels, oversized promotional headings, decorative gradients/glass, fake online counts, cosmetic shops, and imitation Curve Crash branding.

## Principles

1. Get friends from an invite to a match quickly.
2. Make the visible trail agree with authoritative collision geometry.
3. Preserve tight movement and powerup interactions over simplifying gameplay.
4. Keep controls responsive while the server remains authoritative.
5. Expose real status and actionable errors.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-accessible menus, visible focus, remappable steering, reduced motion, player labels alongside color, clear powerup symbols, and readable UI contrast. Desktop gameplay only; narrow screens receive a clear keyboard requirement.
